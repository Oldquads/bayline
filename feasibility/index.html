    Setbacks in each band apply from the band below up to its height. Leave the last band's height empty to run to the top. Starting values come from the zone; check the schedule and any DDO.

    Step 3 · Level stack

    Each group's floorplate is the envelope at its top floor. Apartment numbers are a rough count (net area ÷ average size) until the apartment layout step.

    Step 4 · Core

    Click a library core to swap it into the selected slot. Drag cores on the plan to move them. The yellow edge is the lobby side. Exits follow NCC D2D3 (2 above 25 m effective height) and D2D5 travel; confirm with your building surveyor.
